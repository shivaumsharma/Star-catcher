Star Catcher

A one-thumb arcade game: drag the basket to catch falling stars and dodge bombs. I built it to practise product analysis, and it has no players beyond me. This page is the browser build of the Android game.

What is and is not in this build. No analytics are sent from here: the Firebase plugin is Android-only, so the consent screen you will see inside the game does nothing in the browser (choosing either option changes nothing). The game keeps a private log of taps and rounds in your browser only, and nothing leaves it.

The game engine is a download of about 10 MB and loads when you press Play.
